Furniture workspace — published snapshot

Open https://anakondr.github.io/furniture-viewer/

This repository contains the interactive viewer and generated design assets.
The Python CAD source and development history are maintained separately.
No server is required: controls, geometry switching and previews run in the browser.
Downloads are the original generated STEP, STL, DXF and cutting-list files.
Comparison findings describe differences in the DXF reconstruction, including
edge drilling that is not represented in the DXF machining layers.

The publication date and design fingerprint appear at the bottom of the viewer.
Changing this snapshot does not change the editable Python design.
Three.js is vendored under its MIT licence in vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt.

Left monitor reference: Dell UltraSharp U2711 27-inch, community model by
therealguy419, supplied as SketchUp by the project owner.
https://3dwarehouse.sketchup.com/model/742705ebaa879604f2e5d34c7b677bc4/Dell-UltraSharp-U2711-27
Converted locally with OpenSKP 1.3.0, uniformly scaled to 646.71 mm overall width.
The community stand proportions differ from Dell's published dimensions;
this is a visual reference, not a manufacturing or exact clearance model.
Monitor ghosts are excluded from parts lists, cutting plans and CNC exports.

Right monitor reference: approximate Acer Predator XB271HU 27-inch, modelled
locally from published dimensions and product photographs, not downloaded CAD.
Envelope: 614 mm wide, 551 mm high, 268 mm deep; 27-inch 16:9 active screen.
Housing/stand details are illustrative. This is not an exact clearance model.
Dimensions: https://www.acer.com/ro-ro/predator/monitors/xb1/pdp/UM.HX1EE.A01
Stand reference: https://c1.neweggimages.com/ProductImageCompressAll1280/24-011-082_R09.jpg

BackFlip: one opening 138 mm right of centre, 80 mm from the rear edge.
The body clears the centre frame plank by 10.5 mm. The owner-requested rear
margin is below EVOline's stated >90 mm installation clearance.
Circle80 holes: equal 50 mm margins from the nearest side and rear edges;
centres are 90 mm from both edges. Monitor positions remain unchanged:
rear edges 27 mm (Dell) and 143 mm (Acer) from the tabletop rear.
Projected monitor outlines clear the socket covers by at least 10 mm.
Monitor models are approximate; opened mechanisms and cables are not modelled.
Wood Texture mode shows monitor model colours; other modes keep pale ghosts.
Monitors remain excluded from all manufacturing lists and exports.
